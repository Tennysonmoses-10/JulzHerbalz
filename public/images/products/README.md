# Julz Herbals Product Photography Instructions

Your Google Drive folder (`new images`) contains Canon RAW camera photos (`IMG_3104.CR2`, `IMG_3105.CR2`, etc.).

## 📸 How to Add Your Raw Google Drive Photos to the Website:

1. **Convert `.CR2` files to `.jpg` or `.png`**:
   - Use any free converter (Photoshop, Lightroom, or [CloudConvert.com](https://cloudconvert.com/cr2-to-jpg)).

2. **Save files in `public/images/products/`**:
   Rename and copy the converted photos into this folder:
   - `harmoni-hair-oil.jpg`
   - `natural-aura-bath-powder.jpg`
   - `moringa-bath-powder.jpg`
   - `tan-care-pack.jpg`
   - `baby-bath-powder.jpg`
   - `baby-hair-oil.jpg`
   - `herbal-shampoo.jpg`

3. The website will automatically detect and display your custom product photos on the homepage, product detail pages, and image carousel!
